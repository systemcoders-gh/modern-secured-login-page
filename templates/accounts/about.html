Open source, open learning

Built to be understood.

Modern Secured Login Page is an educational authentication project by System Coders. It demonstrates registration, email verification, password recovery, two-factor authentication, and session management.

Try it thoughtfully

Use a demo email address and a unique password. Follow your local setup guide to access development emails. Do not enter real customer information or reuse credentials from another service.

Your account data

The application stores your account name, email address, password hash, and security activity. Session records include browser details and network address. Authenticator secrets are encrypted; recovery codes are stored as hashes.

Account activity is restricted to your account. Hosting operators control retention, backups, and email delivery. Review the repository's security and deployment documentation before operating a public instance.

Authentication & authorization

Authentication checks who you are. Authorization checks what you can access. This application checks both on the server: a verified sign-in opens your own account pages, and other users cannot access your settings.

A starting point for learning

These controls have limits. Safe production operation also depends on HTTPS, secret management, supported dependencies, reliable email delivery, monitoring, and careful hosting configuration.
